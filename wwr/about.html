Banana

At Banana, our mission is to create safe and memorable river adventures that bring people together. We believe rafting is more than an exciting ride—it is a chance to experience nature, build teamwork, and create lasting memories. Ride the Current. Share the Adventure.

History

Banana began in 2025 when a small group of river guides decided to share their love of white water with more people.

What started with a few weekend trips and a handful of rafts gradually grew into a trusted adventure company serving families, first-time rafters, and experienced outdoor enthusiasts. Today, we continue to focus on experienced guides, quality equipment, safety, and unforgettable experiences on the river.
