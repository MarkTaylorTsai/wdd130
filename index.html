Taiwan

Taiwan is a country located east of China, across a strait. It was once part of China, but was later colonized by Japan for several decades. After World War II, Japan officially signed an agreement renouncing Taiwan, but it did not include any statement returning Taiwan to China. Therefore, Taiwan was taken over by the Chinese government of the time. Later, a civil war broke out, and the government retreated to Taiwan, establishing itself as an independent country there and forming two separate nations with China, neither exercising jurisdiction over the other.
